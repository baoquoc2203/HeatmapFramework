Rules the engine commits to. (1) Bands pre-registered and MECE. (2) Resolution mechanical and single-feed, no judgement at resolution. (3) A forecast is never revised after issue — only superseded by a new ID, with the original still resolving and scoring. (4) Probabilities sum to 100%; confidence reported separately. (5) Nothing outside the horizon influences the probabilities — the October window is excluded from all three live forecasts. (6) Misses are logged in the register before being explained anywhere.

Resolution mechanics. Settled 00:00 UTC daily close on Crypto.com ADA_USDT 1D. Fallback: CoinGecko daily close matched by price value, not date label (its labels run one day ahead of true UTC periods). A close within ±0.15% of a band boundary resolves VOID and is excluded rather than adjudicated.

Scoring — Brier score, skill score, calibration

Multi-category Brier. BS = Σᵢ (pᵢ − oᵢ)², oᵢ = 1 for the realised band, 0 otherwise. Range 0–2, lower is better. Worked for F-ADA-0003 at 37/27/36 — if BULL resolves, BS = 0.5834; if NEUTRAL, 0.8054; if BEAR, 0.5954.

Skill score. 1 − BS/BSref against a uniform ⅓-each reference, BSref = 0.667. Below 0.667 on a single observation beats the uniform baseline.

Calibration. Forecasts bucketed by stated probability against realised frequency — if the 30–40% bucket resolves true about 35% of the time, the engine is calibrated. Reliability and resolution decomposed separately, because a forecaster can be perfectly calibrated and still useless if every forecast is 33/33/33. Discrimination is the harder test.
