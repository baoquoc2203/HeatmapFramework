Amendmentv2.3v2.4 — adopted this session
A9 · Every arming must declare an expiry condition, not only a fill zone A7 required a published fill zone at arming and made WAIT the default absent one. It said nothing about what retires an armed tranche whose zone the market has left behind. Every future arming must publish, at arming, both a fill zone and an expiry condition — a date, a structural event, or a distance beyond which the tranche is stood down. Explicitly NOT applied retroactively: BUY 4 was armed under v2.3 with no expiry and remains armed indefinitely with its zone unchanged at $65,000–$67,000. Retro-fitting an expiry to a live tranche is recalibrating a pre-committed rule mid-flight, which is precisely what was refused for the 1,768 BTC volume bar. The defect is disclosed and carried, not patched.
A10 · The modal-gap metric splits into a direction component and an entropy component Session 47 flagged the single modal-gap number as conflated — a confident disagreement and a high-entropy agreement both produced large negative gaps — and carried the recommendation without adopting it. Recommendation discharged. Every band tree now publishes two independent quantities. DIRECTION: does the tree's modal band differ from persistence's modal band, and by how many bands. ENTROPY: H(tree) − H(persistence) in bits over the published bands. This session's FR-B003: DIRECTION = DISAGREE by 1 band (tree modal B, persistence modal C); ENTROPY = +1.0612 bits (tree 2.0547 bits, persistence 0.9936 bits — the tree is materially flatter). The two sessions are now distinguishable.
A11 · Period-labelling convention is a property of the feed, never of the class of feed A5 established, correctly, that CoinGecko stamps daily rows at period end, and instructed the sibling threads accordingly. The finding was one feed's convention stated as a discovery about feeds. The alignment test must be run per feed, every session, and never inherited — including from a feed of the same type or from this same framework's own prior finding. A12 · The session epoch must come from an authoritative external clock No rule. Z7 · Zone touch semantics — settled candles only Carried as the top framework debt since Session 42 and escalated to "no longer deferrable" in Session 47, with BTC_0001 then 0.61% away and no defined behaviour for a touch. Debt discharged. A zone is TOUCHED when a settled candle's [low, high] intersects its bounds. An in-progress candle may register APPROACH and never a touch — the no-mid-candle rule governs the zone layer exactly as it governs positioning. A touched zone becomes TRAVERSED if the settled candle closes beyond the far bound, or TESTED if it closes back inside or short of it. Applied: BTC_0001 is TRAVERSED (Aug 20 settled candle spanned $68,902.60–$73,410.93 and closed at $73,026.02, above the upper bound of $72,364).
