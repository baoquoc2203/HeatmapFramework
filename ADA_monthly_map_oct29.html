Anchor and horizon. Anchor = the Oct 28 settled close, $0.20607. Horizon = days from the Oct 28 candle to the month-end candle (October 3 days, December 2028 795).

Buckets. up = 1 − Φ(ln 1.25 / σ√t); down = Φ(ln 0.75 / σ√t); within = 100 − up − down (rounded). Thresholds $0.2576 and $0.1546.
