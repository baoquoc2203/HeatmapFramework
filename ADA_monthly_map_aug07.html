Two observations in, the dominant driver of this engine's score is not whether it is right but how sharply it is willing to commit.

Structural implication. Both forecasts were generated from a driftless volatility baseline adjusted by two to four percentage points for structure. That method cannot produce a distribution far from uniform, so it cannot score far below the 0.6667 reference — an approximate ceiling of ~0.58 on a correct call and a floor near 1.4 on a wrong one. The engine is structurally underconfident and therefore asymmetrically exposed to being wrong. Methodology — probabilities, monthly bands, and how buy/sell months are assigned

Volatility basis. Realized daily σ = 3.11% from n=29 daily log returns (Jul 8 – Aug 6), annualising to 59.4%. Rising again as the Aug 6 reversal enters the sample. Scaled by √t: σ at Dec 2028 (877 days) = 92.1%.

Monthly timeline. Driftless lognormal cone anchored at spot $0.19837. Driftless is deliberate: assuming a recovery drift or a cycle-shaped price path is exactly where invented forward targets enter a report, and this framework removed them once already. The median stays flat and all the information sits in the widening bands. Cards show the 25th–75th percentile (±0.6745σ); the chart's outer band is 5th–95th (±1.645σ). June and July 2026 are realised closes, not cone output.

Monthly probabilities. Against two fixed campaign thresholds that stay meaningful across the whole horizon: BULL = month-end close ≥ $0.19 (BUY 3 gate), BEAR = < $0.1480 (death level), NEUTRAL = between. Because thresholds are fixed and the distribution widens, neutral decays from 36% (Aug 2026) to 10% (Dec 2028). Arithmetic, not a view — and the most honest statement on the timeline: the further out you look, the less the middle case is worth betting on.

How buy/sell months are assigned — and what they are not. Labels come from dated cycle anchors only. Oct–Nov 2026 from the 363–376 day post-peak interval off BTC's Oct 6, 2025 top. Jul–Oct 2027 as the 6–9 month pre-halving accumulation regime. Mar–Apr 2028 as pre-halving froth plus sell-the-news. Jul–Aug 2028 as months 3–4 post-halving re-accumulation. Dec 2026/2027 as year-end reduction conditional on a profit existing. Nov–Dec 2028 as markup distribution, the most speculative cells on the map.

No seasonality and no calendar-astrology input was used. Rules the engine commits to. (1) Bands pre-registered and MECE. (2) Resolution mechanical and single-feed, no judgement at resolution. Resolution mechanics. Settled 00:00 UTC daily close on Crypto.com ADA_USDT 1D. Fallback: CoinGecko daily close matched by price value, not date label (its labels run one day ahead of true UTC periods). A close within ±0.15% of a band boundary resolves VOID and is excluded rather than adjudicated.

Scoring — Brier score, skill score, calibration

Multi-category Brier. BS = Σᵢ (pᵢ − oᵢ)², oᵢ = 1 for the realised band, 0 otherwise. Range 0–2, lower is better. Worked for F-ADA-0003 at 37/27/36 — if BULL resolves, BS = 0.5834; if NEUTRAL, 0.8054; if BEAR, 0.5954.

Skill score. 1 − BS/BSref against a uniform ⅓-each reference, BSref = 0.667. Below 0.667 on a single observation beats the uniform baseline.

Calibration. Forecasts bucketed by stated probability against realised frequency — if the 30–40% bucket resolves true about 35% of the time, the engine is calibrated. Reliability and resolution decomposed separately, because a forecaster can be perfectly calibrated and still useless if every forecast is 33/33/33. Discrimination is the harder test.
