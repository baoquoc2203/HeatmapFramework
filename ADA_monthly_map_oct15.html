Methodology — buckets, anchor, cone, and how buy/sell months are assigned

Volatility basis. σ = 4.1488%/day (48 daily log returns, Aug 27 – Oct 14 closes; 79.3% annualised). σ20d 76.3%, σ10d 93.8%. Settled-only; the live Oct 15 session is excluded. Driftless on purpose.

Anchor and horizon. Anchor = the Oct 14 settled close, $0.23018. Horizon = days from the Oct 14 candle to the month-end candle (October 17 days, December 2028 809).

Buckets. up = 1 − Φ(ln 1.25 / σ√t); down = Φ(ln 0.75 / σ√t); within = 100 − up − down (rounded). Thresholds $0.2877 and $0.1726. The edge for "up" is arithmetic: +25% is +0.223 in log terms, −25% is −0.288. Cards show the 25th–75th percentile; the chart's outer band is 5th–95th, clipped at the $0.04 axis floor from about Aug 2028. The fixed-line numbers ($0.19 / $0.1480) were shown once, in the Oct 14 map, as promised, and are now retired.
