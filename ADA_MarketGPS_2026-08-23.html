No seasonality and no calendar-astrology input was used. The labels are also not blended into the probabilities: the narrative never bends the numbers, and the numbers never inherit the narrative's confidence. The contested Wyckoff boundary this session added and removed no months.

Scoring — Brier score, skill score, calibration

Multi-category Brier. BS = Σᵢ (pᵢ − oᵢ)², oᵢ = 1 for the realised band, 0 otherwise. Range 0–2, lower is better.

Skill score. 1 − BS/BSref against a uniform ⅓-each reference, BSref = 0.6667. Positive beats the coin flip.

Hit rate definition. A hit is a resolution landing in the modal (highest-probability) band. Under that definition the register stands at 2/3 and projects to 4/8. The definition is written down so the arithmetic cannot drift, after it did drift once on Aug 21.

Calibration. Forecasts bucketed by stated probability against realised frequency. Reliability and resolution decomposed separately, because a forecaster can be perfectly calibrated and still useless if every forecast is 33/33/33. F-ADA-0009 at 25/47/28 is the second consecutive neutral-modal forecast, and the honest reason is that the structural read is at 50/50 and the bands sit ±0.65σ7d from the anchor. A forecast that says "probably nothing decisive happens in seven sessions" earns little credit when it is right, which is the correct price for not manufacturing a view.
