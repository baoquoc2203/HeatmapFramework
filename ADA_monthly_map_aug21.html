Monthly timeline. Driftless lognormal cone anchored at live spot $0.22979 — itself a live-candle figure, used for the cone only and never for a determination. Driftless is deliberate: assuming a recovery drift or a cycle-shaped price path is exactly where invented forward targets enter a report, and this framework removed them once already. The median stays flat and all the information sits in the widening bands. Cards show the 25th–75th percentile (±0.6745σ); the chart's outer band is 5th–95th (±1.645σ). June and July 2026 are realised closes, not cone output.

Monthly probabilities. Against two fixed campaign thresholds that stay meaningful across the whole horizon: BULL = month-end close ≥ $0.19 (BUY 3 gate), BEAR = < $0.1480 (death level), NEUTRAL = between. Because thresholds are fixed and the distribution widens, neutral runs just 3% (Aug 2026) to 10% (Dec 2028); the Aug bull bucket has swung 61% (Aug 7) → 19% (Aug 18) → 46% (Aug 20) → 85% (Aug 21 am) → 97% now, with ten days left in the month. A 78-point swing in fifteen days is a fixed line being walked away from, never a view changing five times — read it as a distance measurement and never as conviction. Arithmetic, not a view — and the most honest statement on the timeline: the further out you look, the less the middle case is worth betting on.

How buy/sell months are assigned — and what they are not. Labels come from dated cycle anchors only. Oct–Nov 2026 from the 363–376 day post-peak interval off BTC's Oct 6, 2025 top. Jul–Oct 2027 as the 6–9 month pre-halving accumulation regime. Mar–Apr 2028 as pre-halving froth plus sell-the-news. Jul–Aug 2028 as months 3–4 post-halving re-accumulation. Dec 2026/2027 as year-end reduction conditional on a profit existing. Nov–Dec 2028 as markup distribution, the most speculative cells on the map.

No seasonality and no calendar-astrology input was used. Earlier versions of this report assigned monthly calls on "September is historically ADA's strongest month" and similar; those justifications were removed and have not returned. Critically, the labels are not blended into the probabilities: the narrative never bends the numbers, and the numbers never inherit the narrative's confidence. A month labelled BUY with a 42% bear probability is exactly what it looks like — a planned action in a regime whose price outcome is genuinely uncertain.

Rules the engine commits to. (1) Bands pre-registered and MECE. (2) Resolution mechanical and single-feed, no judgement at resolution. (3) A forecast is never revised after issue — only superseded by a new ID, with the original still resolving and scoring. (4) Probabilities sum to 100%; confidence reported separately. (5) Nothing outside the horizon influences the probabilities — the October window is excluded from all three live forecasts. (6) Misses are logged in the register before being explained anywhere.

Resolution mechanics. Settled 00:00 UTC daily close on Crypto.com ADA_USDT 1D. Fallback: CoinGecko daily close matched by price value, not date label (its labels run one day ahead of true UTC periods). A close within ±0.15% of a band boundary resolves VOID and is excluded rather than adjudicated.

Scoring — Brier score, skill score, calibration

Multi-category Brier. BS = Σᵢ (pᵢ − oᵢ)², oᵢ = 1 for the realised band, 0 otherwise. Range 0–2, lower is better. Worked for F-ADA-0003 at 37/27/36 — if BULL resolves, BS = 0.5834; if NEUTRAL, 0.8054; if BEAR, 0.5954.

Skill score. 1 − BS/BSref against a uniform ⅓-each reference, BSref = 0.667. Below 0.667 on a single observation beats the uniform baseline.

Calibration. Forecasts bucketed by stated probability against realised frequency — if the 30–40% bucket resolves true about 35% of the time, the engine is calibrated. Reliability and resolution decomposed separately, because a forecaster can be perfectly calibrated and still useless if every forecast is 33/33/33. Discrimination is the harder test.
